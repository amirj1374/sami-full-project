# Definition of Ready

An item is `ready` only when:

- the problem and repository evidence are current;
- scope and explicit exclusions are defined;
- dependencies exist and blockers are resolved;
- required business/architecture decisions are recorded;
- tenant, permission, privacy and security impact is assessed;
- database/migration and backward-compatibility impact is assessed;
- API/frontend/integration contracts are understood;
- acceptance criteria and test strategy are executable;
- validation commands and rollback limitations are identified;
- an owner can implement it without inventing business requirements.
